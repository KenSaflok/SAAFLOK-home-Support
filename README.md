## Getting Started

SAAFLOK provides smart home automation and security solutions for residential users. Follow the steps below to activate your service:

1. **Create your account** – Visit [saaflok.com](https://saaflok.com) and register with your email address.
2. **Download the SAAFLOK app** – Available on iOS and Android.
3. **Connect your hub** – Plug the SAAFLOK hub into your router and follow the in-app pairing instructions.
4. **Add your devices** – Use the app to discover and add sensors, cameras, locks, and other smart-home devices.

## Installation & Setup

### Hub Setup

1. Unbox the SAAFLOK hub and connect it to your router using the supplied Ethernet cable **or** via Wi-Fi through the app.
2. Plug in the USB-C power adapter. The LED will flash white while booting.
3. Open the SAAFLOK app → **Add Device** → **Hub** and follow the on-screen steps.
4. Once paired, the LED turns solid green.

### Adding Devices

- In the app, tap **＋ Add Device** and select the device category.
- Follow the device-specific pairing instructions shown in the app.
- Assign each device to a **Room** for easier control.

### Automations & Schedules

- Go to **Automations** in the app to create rules (e.g., "Turn on porch light at sunset").
- Use **Scenes** to group actions (e.g., "Good Night" scene locks doors and dims lights).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: I forgot my password. How do I reset it?**
A: On the login screen tap **Forgot Password**, enter your email, and follow the reset link sent to your inbox.

**Q: Can I share access with other household members?**
A: Yes. Go to **Settings → Home Members → Invite** and enter their email address. They will receive an invitation to join your home.

**Q: How many devices can I connect?**
A: The standard home plan supports up to 50 devices. Contact support if you need to expand beyond this limit.

**Q: Does SAAFLOK work without internet?**
A: Basic local automations continue to run when the internet is unavailable, but remote access and cloud features require an active connection.

**Q: My hub LED is red. What does that mean?**
A: A solid red LED indicates no internet connection. Check your router and ensure the hub's Ethernet cable (or Wi-Fi credentials) are correct.

## Troubleshooting

### Hub is not discovered during setup

- Ensure your phone is connected to the **2.4 GHz** Wi-Fi band during initial setup.
- Temporarily disable VPN and ad-blockers on your phone.
- Restart the hub by unplugging it for 10 seconds, then try again.

### Device keeps going offline

- Move the device closer to the hub or add a SAAFLOK range extender.
- Check for interference from other wireless devices (microwaves, cordless phones).
- Re-pair the device: in the app, tap the device → **Settings** → **Re-pair**.

### App shows "Unable to connect to hub"

1. Confirm the hub LED is solid green (online).
2. Force-close and reopen the SAAFLOK app.
3. Check that your phone and hub are on the same network.
4. Restart your router, then wait 2 minutes before retrying.

### Automation / Scene not triggering

- Verify the trigger conditions (time, sensor state) are correctly configured.
- Check that all devices in the automation are online.
- Ensure **Do Not Disturb** mode is not preventing notifications.
